# Vendor and Outsourcing Policy (Northwind Payments GmbH - fictional, lab use only)

Version 1.4 - Owner: Outsourcing Officer

## 1. Critical vendors
A vendor is critical if it supports a payment service, stores customer data, or its failure would stop a core process for more than 4 hours.

## 2. Register
All vendors are recorded in the outsourcing register. Critical vendors are reviewed every 12 months.

## 3. Due diligence
Before signing, the Outsourcing Officer checks financial stability, information security certification (for example ISO 27001) and sub-outsourcing chains.

## 4. Exit plans
Every critical vendor must have a documented and tested exit plan. Exit plans are tested at least once every 24 months.

## 5. Incidents at vendors
Vendors must notify Northwind Payments of any incident affecting our services within 2 hours. Such incidents are handled under the Incident Management Policy.
