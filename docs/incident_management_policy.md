# Incident Management Policy (Northwind Payments GmbH - fictional, lab use only)

Version 2.1 - Owner: Head of Operational Risk

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all ICT and operational incidents affecting payment services, customer data or critical vendors.

## 2. Severity classification
- Minor: no customer impact, resolved within 2 hours.
- Significant: customer impact below 1,000 clients or service degradation under 4 hours.
- Major ICT incident: customer impact of 1,000 clients or more, payment outage of 2 hours or longer, or any confirmed data breach.

The Incident Manager must classify every incident within 1 hour of detection.

## 3. Escalation
- Major ICT incidents are escalated immediately to the CISO and the Head of Operational Risk.
- The Management Board is informed within 2 hours of classification.

## 4. Regulatory reporting (major ICT incidents only)
### 4.1 Deadlines
- Initial notification to BaFin: within 4 hours of classification as major, and no later than 24 hours after detection.
- Intermediate report: within 72 hours of the initial notification.
- Final report: within 1 month of the intermediate report.

### 4.2 Approval
Every regulatory notification must be approved by the Head of Operational Risk before submission. If the Head of Operational Risk is unavailable, the Chief Risk Officer (CRO) approves instead.

## 5. Post-incident review
A root cause analysis is completed within 10 business days after the incident is closed and is recorded in the loss event database.
